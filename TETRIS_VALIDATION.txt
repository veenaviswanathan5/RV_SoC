Checked behavior: four cells per piece/rotation; boundaries; seven-piece bags;
one through four line compaction and separated full lines; wall kicks and blocked
rotation; hard drop and scoring; four-line score; blocked spawn/game over;
restart/best score; delayed auto-repeat; pause/resume; 32-bit timer wrap;
grounded lock delay; edge-triggered hard drop; speed floor; score saturation;
renderer clipping; randomized-sequence board/active-piece invariants.

NOT RUN HERE: RISC-V cross-compilation, Verilator game execution, timer probe
on the user's timer-modified wrapper, or live keyboard/SDL gameplay. Those tools
are not installed on this host. scripts/verify_tetris.sh supplies these checks
for WSL, and scripts/build_tetris.sh supplies the complete build.

No claim of perfection, full ISA compliance, or official Tetris rules is made.
The current CPU baseline passed strict lint and its original smoke test in the
user's supplied logs; that evidence is separate from this new firmware.
